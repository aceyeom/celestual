# celestual: the reel

`celestual-reel.mp4`: 35 seconds, 1080 by 1920, 30 frames a second, with
sound effects only and no music, so a song can be laid over it.
`assets/poster.jpg` is its frame at 14.2 seconds.

## The story

One story, told once, in the phone's own world. lin writes the thing never
said and sends it privately. It waits. Every mutual is revealed on
saturday at nine, and in the week somebody's note comes in. At nine the two
find each other, their two lights go out together over the whole screen,
and out of them comes lin's letter, which turns over: on its other side is
kai's, to lin, read for the first time. It is mutual. The ones that never
meet go out, and nobody knows. Then the question, to whoever is watching,
and the name.

Only lin is ever seen writing. kai's note is first read when lin reads it.

## Style

**Voice.** Everything lowercase. No exclamation marks, no emoji. Only true
claims: a recipient is never told a note exists.

**One world.** The whole film lives on the phone's screen: the letters are
phone screens, the dark is a screen's panel of cells, and the type is the
phone's own pixel face.

**Type.** Jersey 10, cut into the screen's cells, a pixel of the face seven
of ours, fourteen for the words that carry a scene. Each word lights on its
moment as a screen's pixels light, swept across in a few frames, and goes
the same way. Each scene sets its words its own way on one grid: the hall's
two lines staggered, `privately.` twice the size of the line before it,
the night's words centred in the sky, the clock large, `it's mutual.` large
over the letter, the ones that never meet flush right, the question
centred with its last word large, the name in the middle. Everything stays
clear of Instagram's buttons on the right and its caption at the foot.

**Colour.** The ground is black. The colour is the screens' own: each
letter's backlight, lin's ice blue, kai's amber (lifted to a pale gold so it
never reads as brown). Dim light goes cool and grey, as the eye sees at
night. The panel is kept a little darker under each scene's words.

**The hall.** Some three thousand screens drawn from 125 different letters
(`assets/wall-letters.jpg`), no letter near itself and no colour beside
itself, on a curve that rises out of sight, softened with depth and hazed
with distance. One in fourteen is dead. The camera is held as a hand holds
one: a slow unsteadiness, and moves that land a little past and settle.

**The light.** The dark is never only dark. Every note's light spreads
round it as ink spreads in water, quickly and then slower, in smoky
streaks and fine threads that wind along spirals, never along spokes, so
its edge is never a ring. Under it the screen's cells are a quiet night of
stars: a point where a little light has come, a cross and a sparkle where
more has, each twinkling in its own time. Nothing reads as smoke, flame
or a spinner.

**The letter.** In the reveal the letter is a pane of glass the light
moves: it comes out of the light with its words swimming and coming clear,
turns over as a hand turns a thing, and kai's melts back into the light
the way the light moves, edges first, never as burning paper.

**The photographs.** Two black and white photographs
(`assets/photo-sea.jpg`, `assets/photo-shore.jpg`) lie over the panel,
graded to their scene (the blue of night, warm for the reveal, the grey of
mist), faded in from the dark above so the words sit on the dark, the
screen's light passing through their light parts and their people dark
against it.

**Motion.** Nothing moves as a machine would. Every word comes on a little
early or late; the two notes come together on curves, kai's moving first;
each light comes on and goes out in its own time. Scenes cut, never
dissolve. One glass, lin's, is seen at the start and, turned over, at the
reveal, and never another.

**Finish.** Every frame is finished as a print would be: a faint lens veil,
a red rim of halation round the brightest light, a lifted black and rolled
off white, and a fresh grain every frame. The thirty frames a second cut
blends each pair of sixty, as a shutter open half the frame blurs what
moves.

## Sound

`assets/sound.wav` is the film's soundtrack: sound effects only, kept
subtle, nothing musical and nothing under the typing.

- the phone's keys click as the letters are typed
- a soft concert bass drum and a gong as the camera falls back to the wall,
  and a bowed cymbal as the wall lights
- a cymbal swelling into the lens, and the drum and a darker gong through it
- an ocean drum as the sea
- a soft drum as the two touch; a cymbal swell, drum and gong as the
  lights go out together; a bowed cymbal as kai's letter melts
- a soft gong under the question and the name

The gong is played a little slower than recorded so it rings in B. The
whole sits near -29 LUFS with its loudest moment near -20 dB, leaving room
for a song.
