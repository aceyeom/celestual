# celestual: the second reel

`celestual-reel-2.mp4`: 30 seconds, 1080 by 1920, 30 frames a second, with
sound effects only and no music, so a song can be laid over it.
`celestual-reel-2-b.mp4` and `celestual-reel-2-c.mp4` are the same film
with its first four seconds told another way (the hooks, below).
`assets/poster.jpg` is its frame at 1.6 seconds.

## The idea

The first reel is told by the one who writes. This one is told by the one
written to, and it explains what the first never did: the wall from the
other side, the reply, and the guess.

sam finds a letter on the wall that can only be about sam. The wall is
letters to people who don't know yet. Nobody knows who wrote it. sam hearts
it and writes back, and the reply is marked as from the one it was to. Then
sam guesses who wrote it and tells the guess privately. If the guess is
right and they sent one too, both find out. If it is wrong, nobody ever
knows sam guessed.

The first reel asks the viewer to be brave. This one asks nothing of them
at first, only whether they are on the wall, and its last line asks for
the cheapest first step in the product: read the wall.

## The hooks

The first four seconds, the only frames that differ:

- **a**, `celestual-reel-2.mp4`: `you might be on here` / `and not know it.`
  Curiosity and a little vanity. The letter under it does the rest.
- **b**, `celestual-reel-2-b.mp4`: `is this one` / `about you?` A direct
  question, shorter, for feeds where the first second has to work alone.
- **c**, `celestual-reel-2-c.mp4`: no caption. The letter is the hook, for
  the viewers who read the letter before any caption.

Run them against each other. The rest of the film is the same, so any
difference in the numbers comes from the hook.

## Style

**One campaign.** Everything the first reel's DESIGN.md says of its world
holds here: the words in the phone's own pixel face cut into the screen's
cells and lit as pixels light, the wall made of the product's own screens,
the dark a screen's panel of cells, the camera held as a hand holds it,
scenes cut and never dissolve, and the same print finish (veil, halation,
lifted black, a fresh grain every frame, the thirty mixed from sixty).

**What is new.** The interface is the choreography. The heart's soft key,
the replies' sheet, the `recipient` mark, the composer's counter and
`send`, and the mutual's own pixel telling are the product's real parts,
drawn by its own components, so the film explains by showing what the
viewer will touch.

**Colour.** The first reel was ice and amber on night blue. This one is rose
(sam's letter), white (the guess) and acid (noor's note, the one bright
thing in the panel when it comes). No photographs, so nothing in the film
needs anyone's permission.

**The scroll.** The camera stays at a letter's reading distance and moves
along the wall, not away from it, so it reads as a thumb scrolling the
wall. That keeps it distinct from the first reel's pull back into the hall.
Each flick lands a little past and settles, and moves fast enough to blur.
Each letter it lands on is laid over with the product's own screen as it
settles, so the letters read sharp.

**Truth.** Only true claims. Letters are to a first name, never a
description. Nobody is named in a reply, and the reply of the one a letter
is to is marked so. A private note is read only if it is mutual. A guess
that is wrong is never seen by anyone.

**Safe zones.** Every word stays clear of Instagram's buttons on the right
and its caption at the foot.

## Sound

`assets/sound.wav` is the soundtrack: effects only, the first reel's
recordings and hall, nothing under the typing, all of it on B.

- a soft drum and a low gong as the letter is there
- a bowed cymbal as the wall wakes; each flick a tick and a breath of cymbal
  drawn in to where it lands
- the heart: the key and a soft drum, felt more than heard
- the thread coming up: a cymbal drawn in; the keys of the reply
- the cut to the dark: the drum and a darker gong; the keys of the guess;
  the phone going out drawn in to its point
- the week: the ocean drum, low; noor's note, a soft drum; the touch, a
  swell landing on the drum and the gong; a bowed cymbal over the telling
- the wrong guess: almost nothing, and its light drawn in, reversed
- the wall, a bowed cymbal; the name, a soft drum and a soft gong, ringing

It sits near -30 LUFS, the first reel's level, leaving room for a song.

## How it was made

The film is drawn by the studio that made the first reel. It is kept on
the `claude/campaign-studio` branch, and `studio.patch` adds the second reel
to it:

- `app/src/studio/parts/reel2-time.js`: the clock, the words and the hooks
- `app/src/studio/parts/reel2.jsx` and `reel2.css`: the film
- `app/src/studio/films/reel-2*.jsx`: the three cuts
- `scripts/reel2-sounds.mjs`: the sound
- `scripts/reel2-render.mjs`: the photographing and the finish

It reuses the first reel's words, envelopes and screen-going-out from
`reel.jsx`, which the patch only exports, its wall (`wall-gl.js`) and its
sheet of letters, and its finish (`studio-finish.mjs`). To make it again,
apply the patch to that branch, run `npm --prefix app install`, then run
`node scripts/reel2-render.mjs`.
