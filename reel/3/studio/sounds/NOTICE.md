## The sounds alone

The other recordings here are used by `scripts/studio-sounds.mjs`. Each is
a few seconds of one file of the Versilian Community Sample Library by
Versilian Studios LLC (github.com/sgossner/VCSL, at commit `c1ea7bc`),
which is dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal: "you can
do whatever you want with these sounds (even make commercial software), no
royalties, no credit, no special terms". Its pipe organ was sampled by
Simon Dalzell of Ivy Audio, and credit to him and to Versilian Studios is
encouraged. Each was cut from the start given, faded out at its end, and
saved at 48 kHz as Ogg Vorbis; nothing else was done to it here.
