# C. Korean legal and commerce setup: facts and questions for a lawyer

Prepared 15 Oct 2026 by a research agent. **Not legal advice.** This file gathers facts, sources and questions so that a Korean lawyer (military/병역 matters plus e-commerce/tax) can advise quickly.

**Context.** Two founders are on mandatory service until about April 2027. Their category is not known: 현역병, 사회복무요원, or 산업기능요원/전문연구요원. Their plans are:
1. A free web app where two people exchange sealed letters that open together.
2. Later, a personalised brass watch-style locket (the couple's photo inside, engraving) sold in Korea. It would start with a Wadiz or Tumblbug presale, then move to a store. A non-serving relative or co-founder might own the store.

## 1. Ten key findings

| # | Finding | Tag / conf. |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **현역병 count as "군인".** They may not do profit-making work, and any other job needs permission from the Minister of National Defense (군인복무기본법 제30조). 시행령 제19조 lists what counts: (a) running a commercial, industrial or financial business yourself; (b) being a director, auditor, unlimited partner, manager or **promoter (발기인)** of a for-profit company; (c) investing in a company related to your own duties; (d) any other activity aimed at continuous financial gain. | [S] M-H |
| 2 | **사회복무요원** fall under 병역법 제33조 ②. They may not do profit-making work, and a side job needs the 복무기관장's permission. Each breach brings a 경고 and adds 5 days to service. The Constitutional Court upheld this (2019헌마535, 2022). Permits are for livelihood need, welfare recipients, unpaid volunteering for non-profits, or "unavoidable" cases, and each lasts at most 6 months. | [S] M-H |
| 3 | **산업기능요원 and 전문연구요원** "may not hold any other job" (병역법 시행령 제83조 ①). 관리규정 제37조 gives the details, for example no company-officer roles. | [S] M |
| 4 | **Passive stock investing** is widely treated as allowed for soldiers, because it is not "업무". Running a business, being a 발기인 or officer, or earning recurring revenue is not allowed. Preparing a business has itself been disciplined: an NCO was demoted and a court upheld it. | [S] M |
| 5 | **A relative as a front (명의대여).** If the aim is to evade tax or enforcement, 조세범 처벌법 제11조 applies: up to 2 years or ₩20M for the user, and up to 1 year or ₩10M for the lender. The VAT penalty is **2% of sales (1% for 간이과세자)** for supplies from 1 Jan 2025. The operator's spouse is excluded from this penalty, but other relatives are not. Military discipline looks at who is really involved, not at whose name is on the registration. | [S] H/M |
| 6 | **간이과세 threshold.** Since 1 Jul 2024, a business qualifies with annual sales (VAT included) **under ₩104M** (₩48M for real-estate rental and entertainment venues). The value-added rate for retail is **15%**, so VAT is about 1.5% of sales. No VAT is payable on sales under ₩48M, but a return must still be filed. Location-based exclusion zones were redrawn on 1 Jan 2026. Any change for 2027 is unconfirmed. | [S] H/M |
| 7 | **통신판매업 신고.** It is not required for a 간이과세자 or a seller with **fewer than 50 transactions in the previous year**. Otherwise, file on 정부24 with a 구매안전서비스 이용확인증 and pay 등록면허세 of ₩40,500, ₩22,500 or ₩12,000 a year, depending on the area. | [S] M-H |
| 8 | **Naver and Coupang fees.** **Naver SmartStore** (from 2 Jun 2025): a 판매수수료 of 2.73% excluding VAT (3% including VAT), or 0.91% for orders through your own marketing link, plus a 주문관리수수료 of 3.63% at the general tier. **Coupang:** about 10.5% for fashion accessories, plus ₩55,000 a month once monthly sales reach ₩1M. | [S] M |
| 9 | **Withdrawal right for custom goods.** Personalised goods can be excluded from the 7-day withdrawal right only when all of these hold: the item is made individually to the order; withdrawal would cause the seller serious, irreparable harm; and the seller gave a **separate prior notice** and got the buyer's **written or electronic consent** (전자상거래법 제17조 ② 6호; 시행령 제21조). Defective or misdescribed goods can still be returned (within 3 months, or 30 days from discovery). | [K] M-H |
| 10 | **Product safety.** I believe adult jewellery (장신구) is a **안전기준준수대상 생활용품**. That would mean no KC certification, but the item must still meet the 장신구 limits on lead, cadmium and nickel release, and the labelling rules. **Free-machining brass usually contains about 1.8–3.7% lead**, far above jewellery limits (the EU limit is 0.05%). Specify lead-free brass and nickel-free plating, then test. | [K] L-M |

### A5. 간이 vs 일반 (illustration; rates [S], arithmetic mine)

- **간이과세자.** VAT = sales (VAT included) × 15% × 10%, minus credits. On ₩50M of sales that is ₩750,000, before:
  - 0.5% of purchases backed by tax invoices [K M];
  - the card-sales credit (1.3%, capped at ₩10M a year; [K] L, check that it still applies).

  The net is often small. Under ₩48M of sales, no VAT is payable, but a return must still be filed.
- **Limits of 간이** [K, M]:
  - No refund of input VAT.
  - **New 간이 businesses, and those under ₩48M, cannot issue 세금계산서**, only receipts. This matters for B2B or corporate-gift orders.
  - The business moves to 일반 automatically once sales pass the threshold.
- **일반과세자.** VAT = 10% of sales (excluding VAT), minus input VAT on purchases and platform fees. Platforms issue tax invoices for their fees.
- **Income tax** [S, M-L]: code 525101 has a 단순경비율 of about 86%. Rates are set by year; check on 홈택스.

## C. Military rules: summary and questions for the lawyer

### C1. The rules by category

**현역병 (including 상근예비역): legally "군인"** [S]
- **군인의 지위 및 복무에 관한 기본법 제30조 ①:** a soldier may not do profit-making work outside military duties, and may not hold another job without the Minister of National Defense's permission. **②:** a Presidential Decree sets the scope of profit-making work. The current Act (per LBOX) is 법률 제20539호, promulgated 3 Dec 2024 and in force from 4 Jun 2025. Sources: law.go.kr, lbox.kr. **M-H.**
- **시행령 제19조:** a soldier may not do the following where it would lower efficiency, unduly affect military duties, gain interests contrary to the State, or harm the military's honour:
  1. running a commercial, industrial, financial or other profit-making business oneself;
  2. becoming a director, auditor, unlimited partner, manager, **promoter (발기인)** or other officer of a for-profit private company;
  3. investing in another person's company that relates to one's own duties;
  4. any other work aimed at continuous financial gain.

  **시행령 제20조:** permission for another, non-profit job is given only if it does not hinder military duties. Source: law.go.kr (the summary says this version took effect on 8 Jan 2026). **M.**
- **Discipline for enlisted soldiers** [K, M]: 군인사법 제57조 ② lists 강등, 군기교육, 감봉, 휴가단축, 근신 and 견책. The 영창 penalty was abolished in 2020.

**사회복무요원 (보충역; not "군인")** [S]
- **병역법 제33조 ②:** profit-making work and unpermitted side jobs are prohibited. Each breach brings a 경고, and **each 경고 adds 5 days of service**. The Constitutional Court upheld this in 2019헌마535 (2022) and in 2019헌마938. Sources: lawnb, casenote. **M-H.**
- **사회복무요원 복무관리규정 제28조**, as summarised by 찾기쉬운 생활법령 and a 경기도교육청 manual of June 2025. **M-H.**
  - The 복무기관장 may permit a side job, after weighing its effect on duties, where:
    1. it is needed for the person's or the family's livelihood;
    2. the person receives welfare, is near-poor, or receives single-parent support;
    3. it is unpaid social service or public-interest work run by a non-profit;
    4. the head considers it otherwise unavoidable.
  - Evidence is required, and each permit lasts **at most 6 months**.
  - A permit can be refused for high-risk businesses, or for work of **more than 6 hours after duty ends**. Some jobs are restricted (professional athlete, entertainer, doctor or pharmacist).
  - Compliance is checked **at least monthly**, and the permit can be revoked.
  - The permit-notice form 별지 제8호의2 was created on 6 Feb 2024 and amended on 3 Feb 2025.
- **2020 precedent:** 병무청 treated streaming and YouTube as 겸직 whether or not they made money. It later said hobby videos could be allowed with the 복무기관장's confirmation. Sources: Hankook Ilbo 2020-05-26, Segye 2020-05-25. **M.**

**산업기능요원 and 전문연구요원** [S]
- **병역법 시행령 제83조 ①:** they must serve in the designated field and may not hold another job. The exception is that a 전문연구요원 at a university research institute may also work as a teaching assistant.
- **관리규정 제37조 (겸직금지 등)** lists the barred roles, such as company officer. Q&A sites disagree about after-hours side businesses. Sources: easylaw, mma.go.kr, bizforms, a-ha.io. **M** for the rule, **L** for side businesses.
- [K, L-M] A breach can lead to cancellation of the designation (편입 취소) and transfer to other service. Confirm this under 병역법 제41조.

### C3. Disciplinary cases and reports [S]

- **Army NCO, demoted.** He ran a company without permission, recruited investors through a website, and took income as head of an association. He was demoted (강등), and the court dismissed his challenge, treating the acts as profit-making or preparation for it. Source: lawyermiles.com/p32 (an undated law-firm page). **M.**
- **Active-duty officer, Instagram.** Paid Instagram promotions by an officer were reported as a 영리행위 issue. Source: Metro Seoul, 2021-07-08. **M.**
- **Actor 손보승, while serving.** He was registered as CEO of a company that sold a product online, and the sales site was closed. The military imposed no discipline, because he was registered only and took no part or profit. Sources: Starnews 2025-12-03; Nate 2026-01-05. **M.**
- **YouTuber 정성하 (사회복무요원), 2020.** He was told to take down even non-profit videos. 병무청 later moved to allow hobby videos with confirmation. Sources: Hankook Ilbo, Segye, Edaily, all 2020. **M.**
- **Law-firm pages on discipline.** They say penalties range from 견책 to 파면, that preparation can be disciplined, and one is titled "helping for one day… can go as far as prison". Sources: jigeumlaw-military.com; captainlawfirm.com. **L.**

### C4. 명의대여 (name-lending): tax and e-commerce risks

**Seen in search results [S]**
- **조세범 처벌법 제11조.** **①** Registering or running a business in someone else's name to evade tax or enforcement: up to 2 years in prison or a ₩20M fine. **②** Lending your own name for that purpose: up to 1 year or ₩10M. The **evasion purpose is required**. Sources: law.go.kr; 조심2024서5664 (2025-03-10). **H.**
- **부가가치세법 제60조 ① 2호 and 시행령 제108조 ①.** A penalty applies to sales made under someone else's name, from the start of business until the real operator is identified.
  - **2% of the supply value for 일반, 1% for 간이**, for supplies from **1 Jan 2025** (previously 1% and 0.5%).
  - "Someone else" means a person who does not run the business on their own account. **The operator's spouse is excluded; other relatives are not.**
  - Sources: korea.kr (2025 changes), lawnb. **H/M.**

**From background knowledge [K]**
- **Substance over form (국세기본법 제14조).** Income is taxed to the real operator. The registered person can still face tax assessments, collection of unpaid tax from their own assets, and changes to health insurance (for example, losing dependent status once a registered business shows income). **M** for the principle; **L** for the insurance detail.
- **Borrowed-name banking (금융실명법 제3조 ③ and 제6조).** Bank transactions in someone else's name for an unlawful purpose, such as evading enforcement, are a crime: up to 5 years or ₩50M. **M.**
- **전자상거래법 제10조 and 제13조.** A store must show the operator's name, representative, address, phone, email, 사업자등록번호 and 통신판매업 신고번호. A figurehead makes these disclosures misleading, and the registered person carries the legal liability to consumers. **L** (analysis).
- **Platform terms.** Naver, Coupang and Wadiz forbid lending or transferring seller accounts. A breach can mean suspension and withheld payouts. **L-M.**
- **Military view.** Discipline looks at real involvement (see the 손보승 case). A front does not protect a soldier who actually runs the business, and it adds tax and criminal risk for the relative.

### C5. Structures to put to the lawyer (analysis, not advice)

1. **Nothing commercial until discharge.** Keep the free app non-profit (once confirmed that this is allowed), file IP if allowed, and only do research.
2. **A genuine non-serving owner.** The relative or co-founder truly owns and runs the store: decisions, money and work. The founders have no role, no pay and no profit share until discharge. Afterwards they join through a 공동사업 arrangement, a business transfer (포괄양수도), or incorporation.
3. **A company (주식회사) founded by the non-serving person.** The founders acquire shares *after* incorporation (not as 발기인), hold no office and do no work until discharge. Use this only if the lawyer confirms that passive shareholding is fine.
4. **The founders hold the brand's IP** and license it to the owner, either after discharge or royalty-free before it. Confirm first.

## D. Product safety: requirement and cost

**[K]: not searched.** Verify on safetykorea.kr (제품안전정보센터 item search) and in the KATS 생활용품 안전기준 for 장신구, under 전기용품 및 생활용품 안전관리법 on law.go.kr.

1. **Status.** I believe adult jewellery (장신구) is a **안전기준준수대상 생활용품**, the lowest tier (**L-M**).
   - There is **no KC certification or KC mark**, and the mark must not be used.
   - The maker or importer must still meet the 장신구 safety standard (hazardous metals and labelling) before selling.
   - Authorities can order improvements or recalls and impose penalties.
   - Products marketed to **children under 13** fall under 어린이제품 안전 특별법 instead, which is stricter and requires KC. Do not position the locket as a children's product.
2. **Limits.** The standard covers lead content, cadmium content and nickel release. **I could not verify the Korean figures.** EU REACH Annex XVII benchmarks [K, M]:
   - lead ≤ 0.05% by weight in any part of jewellery;
   - cadmium ≤ 0.01% in metal parts;
   - nickel release ≤ 0.5 µg/cm²/week for parts in prolonged skin contact (≤ 0.2 for piercing posts).
3. **The brass problem.** Free-machining brass (C36000 or C3604) usually contains **about 1.8–3.7% lead** [K, M], so standard brass would almost certainly fail a jewellery lead limit.
   - Ask for **lead-free brass**, such as C26000/C2600 cartridge brass or bismuth/silicon "eco-brass", with mill certificates.
   - Ask for **nickel-free plating**. Plated parts still need a nickel-release test.
4. **Testing and cost.**
   - Labs: KTR (한국화학융합시험연구원), KCL (한국건설생활환경시험연구원), KATRI시험연구원 and FITI시험연구원.
   - Estimate: **₩200k–₩600k per design** for lead and cadmium by ICP on each material or finish, plus a nickel-release test [K, **L**; get quotes]. Turnaround is about 1–3 weeks [K, L].
   - Crowdfunding reviewers may ask for the report [K, L].
5. **Labels** (on the product or packaging; the standard sets the exact list) [K, L-M]: product name, model, material, maker or importer name, address and phone, country of manufacture, date of manufacture, and cautions (e.g., stop use if the skin reacts; keep small parts away from children).
6. **Related rules** [K]:
   - Online **product-information table** (공정위 상품정보제공 고시), under the jewellery/watch or fashion-accessory category: material, weight, size, maker, origin, cautions, warranty, and after-sales contact. **M.**
   - **Country-of-origin marking** if the product is imported (대외무역법). **M.**
   - **Packaging rules** (포장재 재질·구조): check whether small sellers are exempt. **L.**
   - A **working quartz movement with a coin battery** adds battery warnings and failure points; a frozen dial avoids both.
   - A **passive NFC tag** probably needs no radio certification. **L; confirm.**

## E. IP: filing costs and times

### E1. Korean trademark (KIPO) [K; verify at kipo.go.kr fee pages or 특허로]

- **Clearance.** KIPRIS (kipris.or.kr) is free. Search Korean and Latin spellings and similar marks in each class. **H.**
- **Classes to consider.** **14** (lockets, jewellery, watches) and **35** (online retail of those). For the app: **9** (downloadable software), **42** (software as a service), and **45** (online social networking) or **38** (electronic messaging). **M.**
- **Fees (e-filing)** [**L-M**]:
  - Application: about **₩62,000 per class** (about ₩56,000 if you use only KIPO's listed goods names), plus a surcharge per item above about 10 items.
  - Registration: about **₩211,000 per class for 10 years**, or in two 5-year instalments.
  - Priority examination: about ₩160,000 per class.
- **Time** [**L**]: first examination roughly 12–18 months in recent years, or about 2–3 months with priority examination. That is followed by a 2-month publication period for opposition, then registration. Without priority, expect about 14–20 months in total.
- **Fee reductions** [**L**]: some individuals get reductions or exemptions (e.g., young people, and people on mandatory service for patents and designs). Check 특허료 등의 징수규칙 제7조, and whether a serving founder should be the applicant at all (C6 Q1b).

### E2. Korean design registration [K]

- **No partial examination for jewellery.** A locket is jewellery (Locarno 11-01). As far as I know, **일부심사 applies only to Locarno classes 2, 5 and 19** (clothing and fashion items, textiles, stationery and printed matter). So the locket would need **full examination**, not 일부심사. **M-L**; verify in 디자인보호법 시행규칙.
- **Fees** [**L**]: about ₩94,000 per design for e-filing under full examination (about ₩45,000 under 일부심사), plus registration fees for years 1–3.
- **Time** [**L**]: about 6–12 months; priority examination is available.
- **Grace period:** 12 months after your own disclosure (디자인보호법 제36조) [**M**]. File before the crowdfunding page goes public if you might file abroad, because many countries have a shorter grace period or none.
- **Secret design** (비밀디자인) keeps a registered design unpublished for up to 3 years [**M**].

### E3. United States (USPTO) [K, M; verify at uspto.gov fee schedule]

- **Fees since 18 Jan 2025:** **$350 per class** base application fee. Surcharges per class: $100 for insufficient information, $200 for a free-text goods description, and $200 for each extra 1,000 characters. Statement of use: $150 per class. Section 8 declaration: $325 per class.
- **Attorney required.** Applicants domiciled outside the US must use a US-licensed attorney (since Aug 2019). **H.**
- **Alternative:** the Madrid Protocol, filed through KIPO and designating the US (the USPTO individual fee applies).
- **Time** [**L**]: first action about 6–9 months; registration about 12–18 months.

## F. Consumer law: withdrawal right and presales

**[K]:** statute text is from memory. Verify at law.go.kr (전자상거래법 제13조, 제15조, 제17조 and 제18조; 시행령 제6조 and 제21조).

- **Basic right (제17조 ①).** The buyer may withdraw within **7 days** of receiving the contract document, or within 7 days of delivery if that is later.
- **Exceptions (제17조 ②):**
  1. loss or damage caused by the buyer;
  2. value much reduced by use;
  3. value much reduced by time;
  4. copyable goods whose packaging has been opened;
  5. services or digital content that have started;
  6. cases set by decree.

  For exceptions 2–5, the seller must have flagged the restriction clearly (제17조 ⑥). If not, the buyer can still withdraw.
- **The decree case (시행령 제21조).** Goods made individually to the buyer's order (or similar goods), where withdrawal would cause the seller **serious, irreparable harm**, **and** the seller **told the buyer separately in advance**, **and** obtained the buyer's **written or electronic consent**. **M-H.**
- **What this means in practice (analysis):**
  - Show a separate notice and an **unticked consent checkbox** before payment, and describe exactly what is personalised.
  - Consider allowing free cancellation **until production starts**, because harm is hard to show before then.
  - Keep consent records. Contract and withdrawal records must be kept for 5 years (시행령 제6조) [M].
- **Defects or goods not as described (제17조 ③).** The buyer may return them within **3 months of delivery, or 30 days from when they knew or could have known**, whatever was agreed.
  - Refunds are due within **3 business days** of the goods coming back (제18조).
  - The seller pays return shipping for defects; the buyer pays it when they change their mind.
- **Presales (선지급식 통신판매, 제15조).**
  - The seller must take steps to deliver within 3 business days of payment, **unless a delivery time was agreed**, so state the delivery date clearly.
  - If delivery becomes impossible, the seller must tell buyers and refund within 3 business days. **M.**
- **Reward crowdfunding** [**L**]:
  - Platforms present it as "support" (후원) rather than a purchase, and set their own refund rules. Typically backers can cancel freely before the campaign ends; after that, refunds are for non-delivery or defects.
  - Whether 전자상거래법 fully applies to the maker is debated. The safe course is to comply with it anyway: a clear delivery date, defect refunds, and the custom-order consent step.
  - Ask the lawyer, and read each platform's current maker terms.
- **Dark patterns** [K, M]. Amendments in force from 14 Feb 2025 ban:
  - drip pricing (the total price must be shown up front);
  - preselected options;
  - hidden subscription price rises;
  - a misleading hierarchy of choices;
  - obstructing cancellation;
  - repeated nagging.

  These matter for event pages.

## G. Privacy (개인정보 보호법) [K; verify at law.go.kr and pipc.go.kr]

- **Scope.** The law applies to anyone who processes personal data for work purposes, including a free app (제2조). **M.**
- **Lawful basis (제15조).** Either consent with the four required notices (purpose, items, retention period, and the right to refuse and its consequences), or performance of a contract. Keep marketing consent separate and optional. **M-H.**
- **The partner's photo.** It is the partner's personal data and portrait, and the buyer is not the partner. **M-L.**
  - Option (a): the partner gives their **own consent in the app**, which fits the two-person letter mechanic.
  - Option (b): the buyer warrants that the partner consented, and the photo is used only for production.
  - A face photo is not sensitive data unless it is processed for biometric identification.
  - If the partner asks, you must tell them where the data came from (제20조).
  - Copyright: a photo taken by a studio belongs to the photographer, so ask the buyer to warrant that they have the rights.
- **Children (제22조의2).** Processing data of children **under 14 requires verified consent from a legal guardian**. The simplest rule is 14+ only. **H.**
- **Retention and deletion.** **M.**
  - State retention periods in the privacy policy, and delete data without delay once the purpose ends (제21조).
  - **Photos:** delete after delivery plus a short claims window.
  - **Letters:** delete when the user deletes them, or after a set period; encrypt them at rest.
  - E-commerce **order** records must be kept: 5 years for contracts, payments and delivery; 3 years for complaints; 6 months for ads (전자상거래법 시행령 제6조).
  - The one-year "inactive account" rule was abolished in Sept 2023.
- **Overseas cloud** (e.g., US hosting). Disclose the overseas transfer or processing in the privacy policy (제28조의8), and list processors such as hosting, printing and delivery as outsourcing (제26조). **M.**
- **Privacy policy and breach notice.** A privacy policy is required (제30조), and breaches must be notified within about 72 hours [L-M].
- **Marketing messages** need prior opt-in, an "(광고)" label, and separate consent for sending between 21:00 and 08:00 (정보통신망법 제50조). **M-H.**
- **부가통신사업 신고.** Small operators with capital of ₩100M or less are exempt (전기통신사업법 제22조) [**L-M**].
