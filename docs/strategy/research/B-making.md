# B. Making, packing and shipping the brass "frozen-time" watch locket

Research memo, prepared 15 Oct 2026. Scope: a first batch of 100 to 300 units made and sold from Seoul, with US sales later.

Product assumed throughout: a brass hunter-case watch locket, 34–38 mm and about 30 g, with no movement and hands fixed at a chosen time. It has a cream Roman-numeral dial, a photo under domed mineral glass, a chain plus trigger clasp (bag charm or necklace) and one engraved line on the back. It ships in a rigid drawer box with a sealed paper letter card (NFC chip plus QR backup), an envelope and a wax seal.

## Key findings

1. **No one in Seoul makes pocket-watch cases as a product.** Jongno is a fine-jewelry cluster: about 800 businesses there make roughly 70% of Korea's fine jewelry [K1]. The practical route for 100–300 units is a blank brass photo-locket or hunter case from a Chinese OEM, built on the factory's existing mold with your finish and dial. Seoul then does the personalisation (dial time, photo, engraving), quality control and packing. Casting an original case in Jongno is the fallback; I estimate it costs 2–4× more per unit.
2. **Chinese cases are cheap at the stock level.** On Alibaba, locket-watch pendants list at $1.49–2.50 (minimum order 10–100) and round brass photo lockets at $0.49–0.55 (minimum 100) [C1–C3]. Flip-lid pocket-watch necklaces show about ¥6.5–7.0 on 1688 [C11]. For a semi-custom brass hunter case, budget about $3–8 per unit (est.).
3. **Thick gold plating is expensive at 2026 gold prices.** Gold sells to dealers at about ₩180,000/g [K12]. On a locket with about 45 cm² of plated surface, 1 µm of gold is about ₩15,600 of metal alone (calc.).
   - Recommended base finish: polished brass under a clear e-coat, with no gold.
   - If you want a gold colour, use a 0.1–0.2 µm flash.
   - US marketing rules on gold terms ("gold electroplate" needs at least 0.175 µm of at least 10K gold) would then apply [Q4, background].
4. **Korean import costs.** Duty is 8% for accessories and for chapter-91 watch goods, plus 10% VAT, both calculated on the CIF value [C20–C23]. Goods for resale need a formal import declaration whatever their value [C19, C20]. The Korea–China FTA can lower the duty if you hold a certificate of origin, but I did not verify the rate for these exact tariff lines.
5. **The NFC chip has to go in the paper card.** Metal behind an NFC antenna can cut its read range from about 4 cm to zero [N14], and a closed brass case shields completely.
   - NTAG213 stickers cost ₩250–610 each in Korea [N3–N5].
   - Printed NFC cards cost about ₩3,400–5,000 each [N1, N2].
6. **Packaging.**
   - A custom rigid box at 100 units costs about ₩8,000–10,000 each for a palm-sized box [P1].
   - Bangsan packaging makers default to minimums of about 1,000 [P2, P5].
   - One Seoul maker charges ₩20,000 each at 50 units, and 100 printed cartons cost at least ₩1.5M [P4].
   - Custom sticker wax seals cost ₩350–460 each, with no minimum (orders from 1 piece) [P12].
7. **Domestic delivery.**
   - Convenience-store half-price parcels cost ₩1,800–1,900 up to 500 g [L1, L4].
   - Korea Post counter parcels cost ₩4,000 [L8].
   - 3PL all-in fees for a small order are about ₩2,700–3,450 [L16, L20–L22].
   - Change-of-mind returns are usually charged at ₩6,000 for the round trip [L24].
8. **US shipping and duty, as of October 2026.**
   - De minimis is still suspended, and a statute repeals it in July 2027 [U14–U16].
   - The Supreme Court struck down the IEEPA tariffs on 20 Feb 2026 [U19, U20].
   - The Section 122 surcharge that followed expired on 24 Jul 2026 [U27, U30].
   - Since 24 Jul 2026, a Section 301 "forced-labor" duty applies to Korean goods: 10% or 12.5% net of MFN, about 12.5% in total [U25–U28].
   - A second Section 301 case, on excess capacity, was opened against Korea in March 2026; I could not find its outcome [U33, U34].
   - The most defensible tariff line is HTS 7117.19.90, imitation jewelry, at 11% MFN. Goods of Korean origin enter it free under KORUS (background; confirm with a CBP binding ruling).
   - A Chinese-made case that is only finished in Korea is probably Chinese-origin for US duty, and that matters.
9. **Timeline.** The first 100 sellable units are about 14–17 weeks away, which means mid-February to early March 2027. That holds only if the China order ships before Chinese New Year on 6 Feb 2027, so place the purchase order by about mid-December 2026. Korean Seollal holidays (about 5–8 Feb 2027) also pause couriers.

### 1.2 Namdaemun accessory wholesale (남대문 액세서리 도매상가)

Not researched; the search budget ran out. Background, low confidence:
- Accessory wholesale is concentrated in multi-storey arcades in the market's core and trades very early in the morning for retailers.
- Vendors sell finished costume jewelry (much of it imported, some Korean-made), hair accessories and some components, mostly by the piece or the dozen.
- Many vendors front factories in Seoul or Gyeonggi and take OEM orders, often from a few hundred pieces per design.

Use Namdaemun to:
1. buy finished chains, clasps and charm hardware in small quantities, and
2. get referrals to zinc-alloy or brass casting and plating subcontractors.

It is not a place to get a hinged case engineered.

A related data point: one Seoul accessory factory with its own wholesale/retail store works in zinc alloy. Its terms are a sample fee of at least ₩10,000 per item, a minimum order of ₩100,000 by value, and a new mold from ₩300,000. Unusual platings (black matte, barrel, paint, 24K) have minimum lot charges by weight, and it advertises "domestic, heavy-metal-free plating" [K6, medium].

**Practical notes (background, medium):**
- Fiber lasers mark brass well.
- Engraving through plating or e-coat exposes bare brass, which will tarnish along the line.
- For fixed marks such as a logo, engrave before plating and coating.
- For the personal line, engrave after coating and touch up with clear lacquer, or accept the brass line as part of the look.
- Keep the back wall at least about 0.6–0.8 mm thick where it is engraved.
- At 100+ orders a month, an in-house fiber laser (₩2.7–4.0M) pays back against ₩3,000/pc outsourcing after about 900–1,300 engravings (calc.).

### 2.2 Dial, frozen hands, crystal (est. unless cited)

- **Dial.** A cream dial with Roman numerals, from your artwork, pad- or UV-printed in China: about $0.3–1.0 per unit at 100–300, plus about $30–80 set-up (est., low). Sublimation-printable blanks exist with minimums of 10–50 [C7, C8].
  - If each customer picks the time, print the whole dial (hands included) per order in Seoul on archival photo paper or a UV-printed disc. That removes loose hands and per-order fitting labour (recommendation).
- **Hands.** Stock watch hands cost about $0.05–0.30 a pair in China (est., low). Glue them with UV adhesive and test with a shake. Jongno 4-ga/Yeji-dong, where the jewelry district began [K3], historically had watch-parts dealers (background, low).
- **Domed mineral glass.** About ₩100–500 per piece from Chinese or Korean craft suppliers (est., low). Fit the dome to the bezel within about ±0.05 mm and seal against moisture.

### 2.3 Samples, production time, and the questions to ask

- **Samples (est., low).**
  - Stock samples: unit price plus about $20–40 express, 7–14 days door to door.
  - Custom samples (your dial, finish and engraving): about $30–150 plus express, 10–20 days.
  - A new mold, if you need one: about $300–1,500 and 3–5 weeks.
- **Production (est., low).** 15–30 days for 100–300 units, plus 1–2 weeks if plating or e-coat is outsourced.
- **Put these lines in the English request for quotation (RFQ):**
  - hunter-case photo locket, no movement, Ø36 mm
  - brass, with lead content stated; a test report for lead, cadmium and nickel release
  - nickel-free plating, clear e-coat
  - cream Roman dial from our artwork; hands fixed at 10:10 or supplied loose
  - domed mineral glass on the dial and the photo window
  - back left blank for laser engraving
  - prices at 100 and 300; sample cost and time; production time
  - individual poly bag
  - commercial invoice stating origin; certificate of origin for Korea–China FTA (Form KC)

**Recommended build.**
- Make the letter card two plies of 300–400 gsm paper with a 25 mm NTAG213 inlay between them, or put the sticker under a printed label.
- Print the same short URL as a QR code on the card at no less than 2 cm.
- Write and lock each chip with NXP's free TagWriter app, at about 30 seconds a unit.
- NTAG213 holds 144 bytes of user data, enough for one short URL. NTAG215 holds 504 bytes. Both support permanent locking and a 32-bit password [Q6, background, high].
- If you later want authenticity checking, NTAG 424 DNA supports it at a higher price (not priced here).
- In the box, keep the card at least 1–2 cm from the locket (est.). Test reads with an iPhone and an Android phone through the sealed envelope.
- Cost per card: about ₩600 with an inlay and QR, or ₩3,400–5,000 for a ready-made printed NFC card.

## 7. Quality control and compliance

### 7.1 How plating wears

- Wear starts at high-contact points: the lid rim, the push-crown and bow, the hinge knuckle and the back face.
- A 0.1–0.25 µm flash can show brass at the edges within weeks or months of daily wear. About 1 µm lasts months to about a year. 2.5 µm or more lasts years (background, medium; definitions in [K10]).
- Cheap Korean plating often has no stated thickness [K9]. Write the thickness into the purchase order and verify it with XRF.
- A clear e-coat adds tarnish and wear protection. It can chip, yellow or peel if applied over residue; test it with tape and artificial sweat.
- Raw brass under lacquer will develop a patina where the lacquer wears through. That suits a vintage piece, but say so on a care card.
- Nickel is often used as an under-plate barrier. Specify a nickel-free under-plate for a necklace worn against skin.

- Budget ₩300k–1M for the first set of tests.

### 7.3 Typical failure modes for this product (background, medium)

- **Hinge:** the pin works loose or migrates; the knuckle cracks at solder joints; the lid sags or stops closing flush.
- **Lid snap:** the catch wears and the lid pops open in a bag (critical in bag-charm use); the push-crown spring fatigues.
- **Glass:**
  - the dome detaches when the adhesive fails through heat or cold cycling or a drop;
  - bubbles or dust get trapped under the dome;
  - cyanoacrylate "blooms" and fogs the glass (use UV-cure adhesive);
  - a mis-sized dome cracks under bezel pressure.
- **Photo and dial:** fading under UV, fogging or mould from trapped moisture, the dial print rubbing on the hands.
- **Chain and clasp:** jump rings open under bag-charm load; the trigger-clasp spring fails; links break at weak solder joints.
- **Finish:** casting porosity shows after polishing; plating blisters over porosity; the e-coat chips at the edges; fingerprints etch the brass before coating.
- **NFC card:** the chip is placed too close to the locket and will not read; the inlay is creased in the envelope.

### 7.4 Inspection practice for small batches

- At 100–300 units, **inspect 100% for function**: lid action, snap, hinge play, glass seated, engraving text, NFC read, QR scan. It takes about 2–3 minutes a unit.
- Use AQL sampling only for cosmetics, under ANSI/ASQ Z1.4 general level II [Q5, background, high]:

| Lot size | Sample size (code letter) | AQL 2.5 (major): accept / reject | AQL 4.0 (minor): accept / reject |
|---|---|---|---|
| 91–150 | 20 (F) | 1 / 2 | 2 / 3 |
| 151–280 | 32 (G) | 2 / 3 | 3 / 4 |
| 281–500 | 50 (H) | 3 / 4 | 5 / 6 |

- Critical defects (sharp edges, lid failure, wrong engraving) are zero-tolerance.
- **Before shipment from China:** at minimum, require a video of 20 random units opening and closing, close-up photos, weight and diameter readings, and XRF readings. A third-party pre-shipment inspection costs a few hundred US dollars per inspector-day (est., low).
- Keep a "golden sample" signed and sealed at both ends.

## (e) QC checklist

**Before the purchase order (golden sample)**
- [ ] Alloy declared; lead content certificate or XRF reading for the case, chain, clasp and solder. Target: lead under 0.1% for comfort; under 1.5% if the metal is unplated; never child-directed.
- [ ] Plating and coating: thickness by XRF (gold, if any); under-plate nickel-free; e-coat thickness stated.
- [ ] Coating adhesion: cross-hatch tape test, ASTM D3359 style (background); no lifting.
- [ ] Artificial sweat soak of 24–48 h on 2 units: no discolouration or blistering.
- [ ] Rub or abrasion test on the lid rim and crown: 500 strokes with a cloth.
- [ ] Hinge and lid: 500 open/close cycles on 2 units; no play, flush closure; the snap holds when shaken hard; the crown push stays crisp.
- [ ] Drop: a 1 m drop onto wood ×3 on 2 units; glass stays seated and the lid stays closed.
- [ ] Heat and cold: −10 °C to +50 °C ×3 cycles (car or bag use); no fogging or adhesive failure.
- [ ] Chain and clasp: 3–5 kg pull on the clasp and jump rings (bag-charm use); clasp spring returns; gate closes fully.
- [ ] Lab report: lead and cadmium (ICP) on each material; nickel release (EN 1811) if claimed; keep the report for Korea and the US.

**Incoming at Seoul (every unit, function)**
- [ ] Diameter within ±0.2 mm; weight 30 g ±2 g (spot-check 10).
- [ ] Lid opens and closes cleanly; snap holds; no hinge wobble; no sharp edges (run a finger round the rim).
- [ ] Glass: no chips, bubbles, dust or glue haze; dome seated flush.
- [ ] Dial: print registration and colour against the golden sample; hands at the specified time and glued (shake test).
- [ ] Finish: no porosity pits, blisters, bare spots or fingerprints; even e-coat.
- [ ] Chain length within ±1 cm; jump rings closed with no visible gap; clasp works ×5.
- [ ] AQL for cosmetics on the lot (Z1.4 level II): 20, 32 or 50 samples for lots of 91–150, 151–280 or 281–500.

**Per order (personalisation)**
- [ ] Engraving text matches the order, character for character, checked by two people from the order record. Check depth and legibility; no burn marks; lacquer touched up.
- [ ] Correct photo, the right way up, centred, sealed; no fingerprints under the glass.
- [ ] Dial time matches the order.
- [ ] NFC: written, locked, read-tested on iPhone and Android through the sealed envelope. QR scans and lands on the same URL.
- [ ] Packing: drawer slides smoothly; insert holds the locket; card kept away from the locket; wax seal stuck down; cotton gloves; anti-tarnish strip (optional).
- [ ] Parcel: survives a 76 cm drop on each face (2 units per new pack format, ISTA-1A style; background); label and invoice correct. For the US, the CN22/CN23 shows the HS code, origin and value, with DDP paid.

**Compliance file**
- [ ] Origin evidence (supplier invoice, certificate of origin) and the marking on the item or box ("Made in …").
- [ ] Wording claims: "brass"; plating claims only if they meet the FTC thresholds (US); no "nickel-free" claim without a test.
- [ ] Not marketed as a children's product (no CPSIA or KC children's certification route).
- [ ] Prop 65 position written down (under 10 employees means exempt; marketplaces may still require a warning).
