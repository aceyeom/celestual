# A · Market scan: existing couples' keepsakes

Prepared 15 Oct 2026 for Celestual (brass hunter-case watch locket + free "pair letter"). It covers the seven categories in the brief.

## Method and limits (read first)

- **Source of facts.** Every fact comes from WebSearch result summaries. WebFetch was blocked, so no listing page was opened. Prices, review counts and stock may have changed since the search index captured them.
- **Search budget.** The session's shared WebSearch budget (200 calls per turn, shared by all agents) ran out after about 60 queries from this agent. Two things were never searched: **category 7** (bag charms, Labubu, 백꾸) and a **direct search for "two-person sealed letter" products**. Those parts are marked **UNVERIFIED**. They rely on background knowledge and should be treated as leads.
- **Korean channel coverage.** The search index covers Coupang, Naver Shopping, 29CM and Kakao 선물하기 (Kakao Gifts) poorly. No product-level data was obtained from those four channels. Korean data comes from Idus (아이디어스), Gmarket, 11번가, SSG, Musinsa, brand sites, press and blogs.
- **Currency conversion.** ₩1,480 = $1, as given in the brief. I also assumed £1 ≈ $1.33, €1 ≈ $1.15 and CA$1 ≈ $0.72; these three rates are not sourced.
- **Confidence levels.**
  - **H** = several consistent results, or an official page.
  - **M** = one credible result.
  - **L** = indirect, old, a third-party estimate, or a scraped or mirror page.
- **Marks used in the tables.** "n.d." means no date was visible, so the figure is a snapshot from before 15 Oct 2026. † marks an appearance detail that comes from general knowledge, not from a search result.

## Key findings (decision-relevant)

1. **The exact product exists today only as a cheap commodity.**
   - Amazon and Etsy are full of "custom photo pocket watch" listings at **$9–30**. They are about 46 mm, quartz, with a black-and-white laser-etched photo on the lid, and are aimed at dads and groomsmen.
   - The premium end is a mechanical hunter case with a photo holder at about **$149**.
   - If Celestual reads as a "photo watch", buyers will compare it with the $10–30 band. If it reads as a "locket", it sits next to **$40–130 fashion lockets** (Abbott Lyon £89–99) and **$245+ designer lockets** (Monica Rich Kosann).
2. **No direct competitor surfaced.** I found no 34–38 mm watch locket for couples with a hidden colour photo and hands frozen at a chosen moment, in the US, the UK or Korea.
   - Korea's nearest items are a 4.7 cm promotional photo pocket watch (₩59,000, about $40) and vintage pocket-watch pendants on Idus (₩28,000, about $19).
3. **Products that encode a moment or a place command premium prices.**
   - Under Lucky Stars prints cost **$130–250** (official prices).
   - The Night Sky sells $39 prints and has about **10.1K Trustpilot reviews**.
   - Coordinates necklaces cost $20–70.
   - Truest North, a GPS compass whose needle points to a place you choose, launched on Kickstarter **from $180**.
   - In Korea, star maps tied to a real date and place were not found. Only zodiac templates exist, at ₩14,000–60,000.
4. **Couples pay for an object combined with a message ritual.** Lovebox made about €6M revenue in 2020 (+154%), has sold over 85,000 of its classic model, and costs $130–175 plus an optional $4.99 a month. Long-distance wearables cost $69–140 and all need charging and an app.
5. **The Korean military-couple market is digital and low-priced.**
   - Apps hold the attention:
     - 더캠프 (The Camp, the army's soldier-communication app): 5.8M downloads and 650K MAU (monthly active users) in Sep 2023.
     - 군돌이 (discharge-day countdown app): 3.5M+ downloads and 530K+ MAU, of whom about 10% are 곰신 (military girlfriends). These figures are from the company's own ad deck, Sep 2026.
     - 곰신톡 (곰신 community app): 2M downloads.
   - Visible physical spending is small: custom letter paper at about ₩5,500, snack boxes at ₩35,000–46,000, and discharge-day cake toppers or engraved tumblers.
   - No premium keepsake for 전역 (discharge) surfaced. 곰신톡 already sells branded letter paper on Idus, so an app is already moving into physical goods.
6. **Korean couple-gift price anchors:**

   | Category | Price per item | USD |
   |---|---|---|
   | Couple rings, entry level | ₩20,000–80,000 per ring | $14–54 |
   | Couple rings, mid level (e.g. 로이드) | ₩100,000–300,000 | $68–203 |
   | Photo lockets | ₩25,000–110,000 | $17–74 |
   | 925 silver compass necklaces | ₩59,000–120,000 | $40–81 |
   | Couple watches | ₩33,000–310,000 | $23–209 |

7. **Main gap.** No product was found that pairs a physical keepsake with a two-person letter that opens only when both have written. The parts exist separately: Lovebox (one-way messages), letters to soldiers in basic training on 더캠프 (one-way), Idus letter kits for long-distance couples and 곰신, and (unverified) couple Q&A apps that reveal answers only after both partners reply. Confidence is **medium-low**, because the direct search could not be run.
8. **Operational lessons from competitors.**
   - Sellers collect photos after purchase by message or email, ask for square crops, and charge **£8–$14 photo add-ons**.
   - Korean locket sellers warn that the snap is fragile.
   - Mighty Dainty notes that a two-person photo needs more room than a small round pendant offers.
   - Hammacher's hidden locket watch fits a **22 mm** photo.
   - Celestual's pair-letter flow could double as the photo upload, crop and engraving-proof step that competitors handle by hand.

## (c) Best design references (8)

1. **Charles Hubert dual-opening mechanical pocket watch with photo holder (Etsy, ≈$149).** This is the closest existing form: a hunter case with a photo inside and an engraved polished cover. Use it as the bar for hinge, crystal and finish quality at $100+. Its case size was not captured.
2. **Amazon photo pocket watches (ZFN, WFE, Godchoices, YVD; ≈$27).** Use these as the **anti-reference**: about 46 mm, ticking quartz, a black-and-white photo etched on the outside, aimed at dads and groomsmen. Celestual's differences must show in a thumbnail: a smaller brass case, a colour photo hidden inside, hands frozen at 11:11, and one line on the back.
3. **Abbott Lyon Photo Love Locket (£89) and Written In The Stars Locket (£99).** These show the fashion-locket model for women aged about 20–35: a photo, a "secret" engraving inside, and birthstones, in 18ct gold-plated steel at about $120–130 without solid gold. "Secret engraving inside" maps directly onto Celestual's single engraved line.
4. **silkpursesowsear solid-brass photo lockets (NOTHS).** This is the material reference. Brass with the photo transferred onto the metal reads as warm, vintage and handmade, which is the same material story Celestual will tell.
5. **Truest North Compass (from $180).** It proves that a needle pointing to a place you chose gets press and crowdfunding traction. Its two-needle layout (direction plus distance) and maple-and-glass styling are worth borrowing. Celestual's fixed "points to you" needle is the battery-free, wearable version. One option is to engrave the distance in km or the partner's city on the compass dial.
6. **Under Lucky Stars ($130–250) and The Night Sky ($39, ≈10K Trustpilot reviews).** These model the "encode our moment" experience: date + place + message produce a unique object, and the buyer sees a live preview before paying. Celestual's inputs map one-to-one: the opening date and time (default 11:11), the partner's city and the engraving line. ULS shows a premium price holds when the object is visibly computed from the couple's own data.
7. **Lovebox ($130–175 plus $4.99/month).** Couples already pay for an object plus a message ritual, and an app subscription can sit on top of a hardware gift. It is a good reference for packaging and for the path from unboxing to the first message. Celestual's 11:11 joint opening plays the role of Lovebox's spinning heart†.
8. **Korean local anchors: Idus mini photo locket (≈₩25k) and 925 silver compass necklaces (₩59–120k).** These set the aesthetic and price that Korean buyers will compare against. Celestual needs visible craft (a real hinge, a crystal, an engraving proof) to justify charging 1.5–3 times these prices.

**Operational patterns seen in competitors (worth copying or beating)**
- **Photo collection.** Sellers collect photos after purchase by Etsy message, eBay message or email. Lisa Angel asks for square images or crops them. Amazon sellers advise bright, centred photos because they convert to black and white. One 40 mm Etsy seller sends a design proof before production. The pair-letter flow could absorb all of this: upload, crop to the circle, approve a proof.
- **Pricing.** Photo add-ons are priced separately: £8 at Lisa Angel, $14 at one Etsy seller. Brighton makes personalization free.
- **Durability.** Korean locket sellers warn that the snap is weak. A 30 g brass hunter case with a sturdy hinge is a selling point.
- **Photo size.** Hammacher fits a 22 mm photo in a watch case. Mighty Dainty notes that two-person photos need more room. A 34–38 mm case probably allows a photo window of roughly 26–30 mm (my estimate), so an in-app crop guide for two faces is needed.

## (d) Gaps: what nobody offers

1. **A jewelry-scale watch locket made for couples (M).** Existing photo pocket watches are 40–47 mm and coded for dads or groomsmen. The photo sits on the outside or is etched in black and white. Nobody positions a 34–38 mm brass hunter locket for couples, in the US, the UK or Korea.
2. **Hands frozen at a chosen moment (M-L).** No listing surfaced that sets the hands to a meaningful time. Searches for "time stopped" watch lockets returned nothing. Etsy "time of birth" clock pendants may exist but were not verified.
3. **A compass that points to a person (M).** The market splits into north-pointing engraved compasses with romantic phrases ("I'd be lost without you", "find your way back to me", "No matter where") and a $180 GPS gadget that points to a place. No battery-free, fixed-needle compass set to a partner's city exists in jewelry form, and none exists in Korea.
4. **A keepsake combined with a two-person sealed letter that opens at a shared moment (M-L; the direct search was not run).** No product found joins a physical keepsake to a letter that unlocks only when both have written. Nearby offers:
   - Lovebox: one-way messages.
   - 더캠프: letters to soldiers in basic training, one-way.
   - Idus paper letter kits for long-distance couples and 곰신, plus 곰신톡 letter paper.

   Further analogs, **UNVERIFIED** (background knowledge):
   - Couple Q&A apps (Paired; Korea's 썸원), where each partner's answer is revealed only after both reply.
   - FutureMe, which delivers letters on a future date.
   - Korea's 느린우체통 ("slow mailboxes" that deliver about a year later).
   - The US "wine box / love-letter box" wedding ritual, where each partner seals a letter to open on a future anniversary.

   **Reading:** the mechanic exists in pieces, but the combination appears unclaimed.
5. **A premium keepsake for 곰신 and 전역 (M).** Visible spending is low-priced: letter paper, snack boxes, discharge toppers, cakes and engraved tumblers. Apps hold the attention (millions of downloads; about 53K 곰신 MAU on 군돌이 alone, from its self-reported 10% × 530K). No premium physical keepsake tied to the enlistment or discharge dates surfaced. The 전역 date is a natural default for the opening date.
6. **Date-accurate "our moment" products in Korea (M).** Korean 별자리 (constellation) products are zodiac templates, not computed from a real date and place. The Night Sky and ULS model has no local equivalent, so Celestual's frozen moment would face little local competition.
7. **A physical D-day object (L).** D-day tracking is dominated by apps (widgets). No D-day keyring surfaced, though this may be a search miss.
8. **A passive, battery-free object for long-distance couples (M).** Every long-distance product found (Bond Touch, Totwoo, Lovebox, Filimin) needs power, an app and sometimes a subscription. An heirloom that still "connects" with no battery (a compass pointing to the partner's city, a frozen shared time) is not offered.

**Threat note.** Print-on-demand sellers already make photo pocket watches at $9–30 and could copy the visual quickly. The defensible parts are the ritual (pair letter, 11:11 opening), the brand and community (military couples) and craft details that are hard to copy cheaply (a 34–38 mm brass hunter case, a colour photo inside, frozen hands).
