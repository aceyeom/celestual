## How to read this

- **Source IDs:** `S#` points to the numbered list in section (g), which gives the URL and date of each source.
- **Confidence levels:**
  - **H (high):** a primary or official source, or major press quoting company data, with a visible date.
  - **M (medium):** reputable secondary reporting, or a primary source that is old or undated.
  - **L (low):** a vendor or agency blog, a self-reported claim, a community Q&A, or a single anecdote.
- **PK (prior knowledge):** a fact from the model's training data (up to mid-2026), not retrieved this session. Every PK item is rated L and needs checking at the URL given before anyone relies on it.
- **Search limits:** only WebSearch was used, because WebFetch is blocked. The per-turn search budget, shared with other agents, ran out after 43 queries. ## 0. Key findings (the 10 facts that should shape the stack)

1. **11/11 is already a gifting day in Korea, and 11:11 is our brand mechanic.** In 2025, Pepero Day sales at convenience stores (Nov 1–11, year on year) were up 30.5% at GS25, 32.4% at CU and 120% at 7-Eleven. Character and collab goods drove the growth: they made up 36.6% of CU's Pepero Day sales, up from 23.2% in 2022 [S1–S4] (H). Pepero Day 2026 is **Wed 11 Nov, 27 days from today**.
2. **Soldiers' phone hours conflict with a weekday 11:11 reveal.** Enlisted soldiers can use their phones only from 18:00 to 21:00 on weekdays and from 08:30 to 21:00 on weekends and public holidays. Trainees get 1 hour a day on weekends and holidays [S25] (H). **[Analysis]** So on a weekday a military couple cannot open together at 11:11 am or at 11:11 pm, and Pepero Day 2026 is a Wednesday. 11:11 am does work on these upcoming dates:
   - Christmas, Fri 25 Dec 2026 (a public holiday)
   - New Year's Day, Fri 1 Jan 2027
   - Valentine's Day, Sun 14 Feb 2027
   - White Day, Sun 14 Mar 2027
3. **The niche is countable but shrinking:**
   - In 2024, **223,136** people entered service as 현역병 and similar [S24] (H).
   - Active-duty enlistment is now about **180,000 a year**, compared with 360,000 in 2000 [S21] (M).
   - The pool of men eligible for active duty was **322,674** at the end of 2025, down 29% from 2016 [S21] (H).
   - The Naver **곰신카페 has about 608,000 members** [S19] (M).
   - **The Camp app had 5.8M downloads and 650k monthly active users** (2023) [S20] (H).
4. **Our prices fit the budget.** People in their 20s spend **₩271,600 per anniversary** on average (2023, up from ₩232,600 in 2021) [S13] (H). They consider **₩100k–130k** an appropriate anniversary gift [S14] (M). **[Analysis]** ₩79k (single) and ₩139k (pair) sit inside this range.
5. **Kakao 선물하기 is the default way to send a gift.** About **189M gifts** were sent in 2025 up to 17 Dec, roughly 540k a day, across 640k products from about 8,700 brands [S7] (H).
6. **On Wadiz, pre-launch sign-ups predict success.** Among projects opened from Jan 2025 to Mar 2026:
   - Projects with **1–50 notification sign-ups (알림신청) succeeded 36%** of the time, and those with **200+ succeeded up to 93%** of the time.
   - About **60% of early sales came from people who had signed up**.
   - Projects that posted **10 or more updates (새소식) had a 99% success rate**.

   Source: [S54] (H, company data, correlation only).
7. **Paid cold traffic is expensive for our price point.** Korean Meta Reels ads cost about **₩500–1,300 per click** [S46] (L). **[Analysis]** At a 1–2% purchase conversion rate, that means a customer acquisition cost of **₩25k–130k per first order**. Each Wadiz sign-up bought through ads would cost about **₩7k–43k**. The owned list and community have to carry the presale.
8. **Creators cost roughly:**
   - Micro-influencers with 10k–50k followers: about **₩300k–2M per piece** on Instagram [S31, S32] (L).
   - TikTok is about **30–40% cheaper** than Instagram at mid tiers [S34] (L).
   - **Naver blog review campaigns (체험단)** cost about **₩5k–25k per review**, plus the product [S37] (L).
   - **리뷰노트** lets brands list a campaign for free [S38] (M).
9. **Disclosure rules got stricter on 1 Dec 2024.** Sponsorship must now be disclosed in the title or first lines of a post, and rewards that are conditional or come later (contests, prize draws) also count [S40, S41] (H). From 1 Jun 2026, AI-made virtual people must be labelled "가상인물" [S40, S42] (H).
10. **"22일" is not a monthly couple day.** 투투데이 is the 22nd day of dating, celebrated mostly by teens and fading [S16] (M). The **14th of every month** does carry a themed couple day [S15] (M).

**Legal flag (PK, L):** active-duty personnel face limits on for-profit activity and side jobs under 군인의 지위 및 복무에 관한 기본법. Check with the unit's legal officer before any paid presale, ad spend, or paid creator contract in the founders' names before discharge. A free pair letter is lower-risk. Not verified this session.

### d6. Unit economics derived from the figures above [Analysis]

- **Korean cold traffic, cost per first order** = CPC ÷ conversion rate. With Reels CPC of ₩500–1,300 and 1–2% conversion, that is **₩25,000–130,000**. Breaking even on a ₩79k single needs CAC at or below gross profit per order. **[Analysis]** That is only plausible with CPC ≤ about ₩700 and conversion ≥ 2%, so use paid ads for retargeting and sign-ups, not cold sales.
- **Cost per Wadiz sign-up through Meta** = CPC ÷ sign-up rate. With a 3–7% sign-up rate (S57, blog benchmark), that is **₩7,100–43,300**. **200 sign-ups would cost ₩1.4M–8.7M.**
- **US** = CPM $14–23 at 1–1.5% CTR, giving a CPC of about $0.9–2.3. At 1–1.5% conversion, CAC is about **$60–230**, consistent with the $70–180 jewellery benchmark.
- **Our own past funnel:** 300k views × 0.05% ≈ **150 sign-ups**. Each +0.1 percentage point at 300k views adds about 300 sign-ups. Comment-to-DM is the cheapest lever to test.
