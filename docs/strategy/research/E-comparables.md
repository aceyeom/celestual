# E. Comparables: what similar businesses' numbers teach Celestual

Prepared 15 Oct 2026, for Celestual (a free sealed-letter mechanic that opens at 11:11, plus a paid brass watch locket at ₩79,000 single or ₩139,000 a pair; Korea first, starting with military couples).

## Read this first: what is verified and what is not

- **Verified rows** come from web-search results returned this session. Each carries the URL the search returned and the date when one was visible. I ran about 30 searches before the **web-search budget shared by all agents in this turn ran out.**
- **Background rows** (marked **[BG]**) come from prior knowledge and were **not checked this session**. Their URL is where to verify them, and their confidence is capped at medium. Sections 4, 5, 6 and 7 are mostly [BG]. **No Wadiz amounts or backer counts were retrieved.** The Gaps section at the end lists the exact queries to run next.
- Revenue figures from Grips, ECDB, Owler, ZoomInfo, Alura, Marmalead, Tracxn, Craft and Sensor Tower are third-party models, not filings. Treat them as order of magnitude only.
- Currency conversions use about ₩1,400 per US$ (an assumption, not checked). On that basis ₩79,000 is about $56 and ₩139,000 is about $99.

## Bottom line

1. **Couple apps make very little money per user, even when they win.** SumOne, Korea's leading couple app, makes about ₩6B a year from about 1M daily users. That is roughly ₩6,000 per daily user per year (derived). One ₩79,000 locket brings in as much revenue as about 13 SumOne daily users do in a year. The keepsake is the thing to charge for. The free mechanic should be judged only by how many sealed pairs turn into locket orders.
2. **Two price worlds exist.**
   - Gadgets for long-distance couples (Lovebox, Friendship Lamps, Bond Touch, Totwoo) sell at about $118–139 a pair and plateau at about $3–5M a year.
   - Personalized jewelry that wins on marketplaces sells at about $35–40 an order, in very large volume. Caitlyn Minimalist has 3.9M Etsy sales.
   - Celestual's ₩139,000 pair is priced like a long-distance gadget, not like Etsy jewelry.
3. **The channels that worked here were nearly free.** They were:
   - marketplace search plus reviews
   - one TikTok video (Agapé got 160k+ installs overnight; Locket spread on a long-distance founder story)
   - word of mouth between partners (SumOne took no venture money)
   - paid partners present at the emotional moment (Legacy Touch pays funeral homes $105 on a $480 order)

   Paid social barely pays back at a ₩79,000 order.

## (b) Lessons, ranked by how much they should change the plan

### 1. The free mechanic is a funnel, not a product, and Korea's couple Q&A habit is already taken

- **Evidence:**
  - SumOne: 19M signups, 1M+ DAU, about ₩6B revenue at about 30% margin. That is about ₩6,000 per daily user per year (S43–S44).
  - Between: 26M downloads and 1M+ daily users, yet it was sold twice (S52–S59).
  - BeReal: fell from 73.5M to about 23M MAU and had no revenue before its sale (S76–S80).
  - Lovewick: 450k users with unclear revenue (S70).
  - A chosen-date letter opened at 11:11 happens once per couple per occasion, so it cannot build a daily habit.
- **Plan change:**
  - Build the sealed letter as a light flow that needs no install, or as a minimal app.
  - Show a locket offer at two moments: when the letter is sealed ("seal it in brass", as a pre-order) and when it opens at 11:11.
  - Track one number above all: sealed pairs that turn into locket orders.
  - Do not add daily questions to chase DAU, and do not raise money on app metrics.
  - Treat SumOne as a possible channel (ads, or a collaboration around keepsakes or 반려몽) rather than a rival.

### 2. Make the pair the default and get customers for free; paid social will not pay back at ₩79,000

- **Evidence:**
  - CAC benchmarks: fashion $90–120 (about ₩125–170k); Meta average $212–230 (S21).
  - ROAS overstates profit: in one example 4.5x ROAS was about 1.04x profit on ad spend (S22).
  - Pair-price anchor for long-distance gadgets: $118–139 (S29, S32).
  - Organic launches worked: Agapé got 160k+ installs from one TikTok (S67), and Locket grew on its long-distance founder story (S72).
- **Illustration (assumption, not data):** Branvas puts gross margin at 70–85%. After shipping, payment fees and packaging, assume about 60% contribution before marketing.
  - A ₩79,000 single leaves about ₩47k.
  - A ₩139,000 pair leaves about ₩83k.
  - At a fashion-level CAC, both lose money on the first order.
  - Keeping about 30% of revenue as profit on the pair means a blended CAC of about ₩40k or less.
- **Plan change:**
  - Lead with the ₩139,000 pair. Position the single as a gift for someone whose partner already uses the app.
  - Spend the first months on organic short video: the founder story, the 11:11 opening, and the locket clicking open.
  - Turn on paid ads only once organic converts.

### 3. Sell at the emotional moment through paid partners, as Legacy Touch does

- **Evidence:**
  - Legacy Touch: $480 average order, $105 (about 22%) to the funeral home, 23% follow-on orders, under 1% returns (S37).
  - For military couples, the same kind of moment is 입영 (enlistment), 수료식 (boot-camp completion) and 전역 (discharge).
  - [BG] Letters to trainees already flow through 더캠프, and Korean soldiers have used phones after duty hours since 2020. Both are unverified this session. The letter is no longer scarce, so the ritual and the object must carry the value.
- **Plan change:**
  - Position the product as **"stop the hands at the hour they left; open together on 전역일."**
  - Recruit 2–3 partners on commission or codes (15–20%): photo studios or florists near the training centers, 곰신 community admins, and vendors at completion ceremonies.
  - Interview 곰신 to test whether letters still matter now that soldiers have phones.

### 4. Let the letter become the keepsake

- **Evidence:**
  - Storyworth turns a year of prompts into a $99 book and has printed 1M+ books while bootstrapped (S39–S41).
  - Caitlyn Minimalist started with handwriting pieces (S4).
  - Handwriting memorial jewelry is an established Etsy category (S42).
  - The Night Sky sells a frozen date and place (S23).
- **Plan change:**
  - Capture each partner's handwriting, or one line of each letter, in the app.
  - Engrave it inside the lid, or print a micro-scroll of the opened letters to go behind the photo.
  - This joins the free and paid layers and is hard to copy: a clone cannot engrave your partner's sealed words.

### 5. Plan a profitable small business with a cheaper volume item, not a venture-scale company

- **Evidence:**
  - Revenue ceilings: Lovebox about $3–5M a year (S25–S27); Friendship Lamps over $3M, then sold (S34–S35); Bond Touch under $5M (estimate, S30–S31); Paired about $2–3M (estimate, S64).
  - SumOne grew profitably without venture money (S43).
  - GLDN reached $10M in 5 years (S6). Oak & Luna, at about $17M, is declining (S14), and MYKA was renamed twice (S10).
  - Etsy winners sell at about $35–40 an order in volume (S1–S3).
- **Plan change:**
  - Set the Korea plan around a ₩1–5B revenue ceiling.
  - Add an entry item at ₩29–39k (for example, a brass charm or keyring with the 11:11 dial) for 빼빼로데이, 100-day anniversaries and friends.
  - For the US: list on Etsy with personalization, collect email and SMS, then move repeat buyers to Celestual's own site, as GLDN did.

### 6. Seasonality and inventory: make to order, fund the first batch with pre-orders, avoid stores and subscriptions, and diversify channels

- **Evidence:**
  - [BG] Things Remembered (engraving retail, Chapter 11 in 2019); Alex and Ani and Origami Owl (fad booms and busts); Loot Crate (subscription); TMON and WeMakePrice (Korean channel risk).
  - Lovebox and the other gadget brands depend on gift seasons.
- **Plan change:**
  - Stock only blank lockets in 2–3 finishes and engrave to order.
  - Run a Wadiz pre-order to fund the first batch and test price, but pull the Wadiz benchmarks first (see Gaps).
  - 11/11/2026 is 27 days away and is the natural 11:11 moment. Unless production is ready, use it as a teaser and pre-order date for Christmas delivery rather than a full launch.
  - Brass tarnishes and can mark skin. Budget for coating and say so on the product page.

### 7. Second market: Korean couple products found users in Asia first; US couple apps are much smaller

- **Evidence:**
  - Between had more overseas than Korean users by 2015 (S54).
  - SumOne is No. 1 in 6+ countries and sells its 반려몽 plush in Japan (S45–S46, S49).
  - The largest US comparables are far smaller than SumOne's 19M signups: Agapé 2M, Paired 500k, Lovewick 450k (S65–S71).
- **Plan change:**
  - Keep the US as a marketplace play (Etsy) rather than an app launch.
  - Consider testing Japan, Taiwan or Southeast Asia before a full US push.
  - Confidence here is medium-low. Which countries Between and SumOne won abroad was not retrieved.

### 8. Keep the mutual reveal free and honest, and do not count on the mechanic as a moat

- **Evidence ([BG]):**
  - Datamatch, Marriage Pact and Last Chances are free and supported at most by sponsors.
  - Gas shut down, and NGL paid a $5M FTC settlement over fake messages and paid "who sent it" hints.
  - Facebook copied the mutual-crush mechanic in 2019 (Secret Crush).
- **Plan change:**
  - Keep "11:11 nights" free: no paid hints and no fake matches.
  - Earn from an optional keepsake for a mutual match, or from a sponsoring café, as Datamatch does with sponsored dates.
  - Register the 11:11 dial design (디자인권) and the trademark early.
