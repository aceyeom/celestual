Deleting your data

last updated 3 september 2026

One page, one field, and it is done in a few seconds. You do not need an account, a code, or anything from us.

Go to celestual.us/optout and type the handle.

what happens when you do

  • The handle is barred permanently. Nobody can enter it again, and any attempt to is refused.
  • Every ping pointing at it is erased, on both sides.
  • Every mutual it was part of is erased.
  • Any membership, any pending verification, any recovery record and any sign in link it holds are erased.
  • Any card or letter it wrote is erased with it.
  • Nobody is told. Not the people who had pinged it, and not the people it had pinged.

It takes effect immediately and it does not need our involvement. It applies whether or not the handle has ever been used on celestual, which is the point: somebody who wants nothing to do with this product should not have to join it to say so.

if you change your mind

The bar can be lifted, so the handle can be used again. What was erased stays erased: there is no copy anywhere to restore. Write to contact@celestual.app from an address we can connect to the account, or send a direct message from the handle itself to our Instagram account, which is the same proof the product uses everywhere else.

what happens without you doing anything

A ping stands sixty days and then it is purged unless somebody renews it. A letter on the wall expires after thirty days. Neither needs a request.

what we keep

The bar itself, as a salted hash of the handle, because a permanent refusal that we forget is not permanent. It contains nothing else, and it cannot be turned back into a handle.

anything else

contact@celestual.app. See also the privacy policy for what is stored and why.
