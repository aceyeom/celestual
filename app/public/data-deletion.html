Deleting your data

last updated 8 september 2026

One page, one field, and about a minute. You do not need an account here, and there is nothing to ask us for. Since taking a handle off also erases what other people placed on it, the page asks you to prove the handle is yours first, which is one direct message.

01 What happens when you do

  • The handle is barred permanently. Nobody can place a ping on it again, and any attempt is refused.
  • Every ping pointing at it is erased, on both sides.
  • Every mutual it was part of is erased.
  • Any membership, pending verification, recovery record, session and sign in link it holds are erased.
  • Anything it wrote is erased with it.
  • Nobody is told. Not the people who had pinged it, and not the people it had pinged.

It takes effect at once and it does not wait on us. It works whether or not the handle has ever been used on celestual, which is the point: somebody who wants nothing to do with this product should not have to join it in order to say so.

02 Why it asks whose it is

The page shows a short code. Send it to our Instagram account from the account you are taking off, and the control appears the moment the message lands. It is the same proof the rest of the product uses, and it still needs no account here.

It asks because this erasure is not only yours. Taking a handle off also deletes pings other people placed, and for a while any handle typed into this page could be barred by anybody. The refusal says nothing about whether a handle was ever on the books, so the page cannot be used to find out who is here.

03 A letter on the wall

A letter about you at a participating campus has two ways off, and they are not the same act.

  • One letter. Report it, on the letter. It comes off the wall on the tap, before anybody reviews it, and a person at the desk can put it back if the report turns out to be wrong. Nothing is destroyed.
  • A whole name. The handle comes off the wall and every letter written to it goes with it. Nobody can reverse that, so it asks you to prove the handle through Instagram first.

A letter also comes off by itself after thirty days.

04 If you change your mind

The bar can be lifted, so the handle can be used again. What was erased stays erased, because there is no copy anywhere to restore. Write to contact@celestual.app from an address we can connect to the account, or send a direct message from the handle itself.

05 Without you doing anything

A ping stands for sixty days and is then purged unless somebody renews it. A letter comes off the wall after thirty. Neither one needs a request.

06 What we keep

  • The bar itself, as a salted hash of the handle, because a permanent refusal we forget is not permanent. It holds nothing else and cannot be turned back into a handle.
  • The record of a letter that was refused or taken down, so a decision can be looked at again. It carries no author.
  • Deleted rows can sit in an encrypted backup for a short period before it is overwritten.

07 Anything else

Write to contact@celestual.app and we answer within thirty days. The privacy policy says what is stored, why, and for how long.
