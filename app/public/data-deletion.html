Deleting your data

last updated 8 september 2026

One button, one message, about a minute. You do not need an account, and there is nothing to ask us for.

the way out

Type the handle, send the code it gives you, and it is done. Every ping is erased on both sides, and every letter about the handle comes off the wall.

take a handle off celestual.us/optout
terms privacy deleting your data

01 What it does

  • The handle is barred permanently. Nobody can place a ping on it again, and any attempt is refused.
  • Every ping pointing at it is erased, on both sides, and so is every mutual it was part of.
  • Every letter written about it comes off the wall, on every campus, and the name can never be written to again.
  • Anything the handle itself wrote is erased with it.
  • Any membership, pending verification, recovery record, session and sign in link it holds are erased.
  • Nobody is told. Not the people who had pinged it, and not the people it had pinged.

It takes effect at once and it does not wait on us. It works whether or not the handle has ever been used on celestual, which is the point: somebody who wants nothing to do with this product should not have to join it in order to say so.

02 Why it asks whose it is

The page shows a short code. Send it to our Instagram account from the account you are taking off, and the button appears the moment the message lands. It is the same proof the rest of the product uses, and it still needs no account here.

It asks because this erasure is not only yours. Taking a handle off also deletes pings other people placed and letters other people wrote, and for a while anybody could type any handle into this page. The refusal says nothing about whether a handle was ever on the books, so the page cannot be used to find out who is here.

03 One letter, or the whole name

The button above takes the whole name off both surfaces. For a single letter about you at a participating campus, there is a smaller act that costs nothing:

  • One letter. Report it, on the letter. It comes off the wall on the tap, before anybody reviews it, and a person at the desk can put it back if the report turns out to be wrong. Nothing is destroyed.
  • Every letter, and nothing else. The wall has its own takedown for a whole name, from a verified handle, if you want the letters gone without leaving the product.

A letter also comes off by itself after thirty days, and a ping lapses after sixty. Neither needs a request.

04 What we keep

  • The bar itself, as a salted hash of the handle, because a permanent refusal we forget is not permanent. It holds nothing else and cannot be turned back into a handle.
  • The record of a letter that was refused or taken down, so a decision can be looked at again. It carries no author, and no desk can put a letter back up for a handle that has left.
  • Deleted rows can sit in an encrypted backup for a short period before it is overwritten.

05 If you change your mind

The bar can be lifted, so the handle can be used again. What was erased stays erased, because there is no copy anywhere to restore. Write to contact@celestual.app from an address we can connect to the account, or send a direct message from the handle itself. We answer within thirty days, and the privacy policy says what is stored and why.
