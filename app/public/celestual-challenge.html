Celestual Challenge

Are you confident and ambitious? We want you on the team!

We’re hiring a head of marketing! Instead of an interview, we want to see if you are willing to try. Everyone who enters spends one week marketing Celestual however they want. We post all of it. The people who resonate with audiences and us the most, win a job and equity.

How it runs

  1. Apply! Read through this document, and sign it!
  2. Get your link. Create your personal tracking link at www.celestual.us/trial so we can see the signups you drive!
  3. Make two videos. An intro and a result. Both are laid out below.
  4. Send them in. Email both your videos + this signed doc to contact@celestual.app.
  5. We post and score. Your videos go on our page. We score everyone and hire the winners!

The deadline

Applications are open until August 10! Send both videos to contact@celestual.app. Don’t miss the deadline!

Video 1: The Intro

This is your pitch. Who you are, what you’re going to do, and why it’ll work.

  1. Your hook. Feel free to create your own hook or pick a sample hook below! Try saying it with a straight face, like you really mean it.
    Examples
    • If you were given the chance to own a startup with just a camera, would you take it?
    • While you’re applying for jobs, I’m winning equity in a startup.
    • I’m tired of just working for the company, so why not try and own some of it?
    • The job market is cooked, so why not escape it altogether?
    • If you told me that all I needed to own part of a company was a camera, I’d call you insane, but here we are.
    • Watch me go from broke to owning part of a startup.
    • I’m getting equity in a startup with no résumé, no experience, and no interview.
  2. Make this next step very clear: Tell the viewers that you are taking part in Celestual’s competition and trying to become their head of marketing. Say it your way so the viewer knows exactly what this is for.
    Example “I’m in Celestual’s competition to become their head of marketing, and here’s my plan to win it.”
  3. Your plan. What you’re going to do to market Celestual. Your message should be brief but detailed.
  4. Explain Celestual. A viewer may never have heard of it. Don’t just list the steps — say what it saves you from. In your own words, but it has to land this:
    Example We all have someone we want to text, but refuse to risk the rejection. Celestual lets you find out without the risk. You can put their @ in and they’ll never know. But if they put you in too, you’ll both find out.
  5. Close: “Follow Celestual to see how I do.” Or another similar closing remark.

How to shoot it

  • 20 to 60 seconds, filmed vertical, good lighting.
  • Film however you want, but make sure each choice is intentional. Any choice in filming should be to make the video look better to an audience.
  • Good trendy audio never disappoints!

Cross-post it (optional, but highly suggested)

You can post your intro to your own account as a collaboration with us, so it lives on both pages at once.

Your custom link

Make your custom link at www.celestual.us/trial — e.g. celestual.us/jack. It will let us track the signups you drive. Put it wherever your campaign allows: a bio, a caption, an end card, a QR at an event. If your campaign is a film with nowhere natural to place a link, don’t force it — that’s exactly why signups are only a small part of the score.

Video 2 — The Result

This is the second and final video. It shows what you actually did. It’s posted only on the Celestual account.

If your campaign is content (a short film, a skit, a reel): your result video is that content. Submit the real thing you made and posted. Don’t film yourself explaining it.

If your campaign happened in the real world (an event, a stunt, a table on campus): your result video is the story of what happened. Show the setup, people showing up, the moment it’s working, and what you pulled off. Make a stranger who wasn’t there feel like they were. End on what you accomplished in one line, with real numbers if you have them.

Either way, it must:

  • Be about Celestual and clearly market it. If a viewer finishes and doesn’t understand what Celestual is or want to check it out, it didn’t work.
  • Run at least 30 seconds. No hard cap on length.
  • Land the mechanic somewhere: put their @ in, they’re never told, you only both find out if they put you in too.

Hard rules

Breaking any one of these is a hard drop!

  • No advertising or targeting anyone under 18, and nothing filmed at or aimed at a high school audience.
  • No paid promotion, boosting, or bought views, followers, or engagement.
  • Don’t claim to speak for the company.
  • If the video or the idea is too shabby, it probably won’t be posted on our account. Try your best!

What you win

Win, and you become our Head of Marketing with 1% equity, vesting over two years with a one-year cliff. Monthly pay is discussed directly with the winner.

Everyone who finishes becomes an official Celestual ambassador with monthly pay and gets a signed letter confirming it. That’s a real job. You keep everything you shoot.

Reference — what Celestual is

You put someone’s handle in. They’re never told. If they put you in too, you both find out at the same second. If they never do, nobody ever knows.

It’s not a dating app. You can enter someone as a friend, an ex, a crush, or something you don’t have a word for. The point isn’t romance. The point is that somebody finally says the thing they were never going to say, without the risk of being the one who cared more. Long term, we want to change how people handle conflict and unfinished relationships.

The part you should understand

Celestual only works when the people around a person are on it too. One user alone enters a handle and nothing fires, because the other side isn’t there yet. That’s why, right now, reach matters more to us than signups. A person who joins an empty app leaves and doesn’t come back. A follower costs us nothing and stays. So we’re paying you to make people watch, share, and remember us — not just to fill the app. Understand that and you’ll make better calls all week.
